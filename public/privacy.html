Privacy Policy

Operated by Cruise Shoppers Network, LLC  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cruise Shoppers collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

The Cruise Shoppers website (the "Service") is operated by Cruise Shoppers Network, LLC ("Cruise Shoppers," "we," "us"), Lithia, FL 33547, 813-415-7619. Cruise Shoppers is a marketplace that connects travelers ("you") with independent travel advisors who respond with personalized cruise quotes.

2. Information we collect

Account information you provide when you create an account: your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number, plus your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text).

Quote requests you submit: the sailing you're interested in (cruise line, ship, sail date, departure port), the number of cabins and guests and their ages, cabin-type preferences, any discounts you note, and free-text notes.

Messages you exchange with an advisor after you accept a quote.

Search activity such as the natural-language and filter searches you run.

Usage data collected automatically, such as pages viewed and general device/browser information, through privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to operate the Service: to create and secure your account, to route your quote requests to advisors, to deliver quotes and messages to you, to notify you (for example, when a quote is ready or a saved-search match appears), to prevent abuse, and to improve the Service.

4. You stay anonymous to advisors until you choose otherwise

When you request a quote, advisors see the sailing details and your preferences, but not your name, email, or phone number. Your contact details are shared with a specific advisor only when you accept that advisor's quote, so they can finalize your booking. You are never listed publicly.

5. When we share information

We share your information only as needed to run the Service:

With travel advisors, the sailing/preference details of your request (anonymized as described above), and your contact details once you accept a quote.

With service providers that operate the Service on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations: our hosting and analytics provider (Cloudflare), our email provider (used to send account, quote, and notification emails), and our cruise-catalog data provider (used to power search). Natural-language search is processed by an AI model hosted by our infrastructure provider; we send only your typed search text, not your identity.

For legal reasons, if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Cruise Shoppers, our users, or the public.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use a single, essential cookie to keep you signed in. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless web analytics to understand aggregate traffic and improve the Service; it does not track you across other websites or build an advertising profile.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal information (see below).

8. Your choices and rights

You can review and update your account information at any time, and you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws; we will honor those rights as required.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Contact Cruise Shoppers Network, LLC at Lithia, FL 33547, or 813-415-7619.
