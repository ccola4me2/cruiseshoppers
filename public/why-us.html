Why us

Why Cruise Shoppers?

Cruise Shoppers is a smarter way to book. Instead of guessing at online prices, you have trusted travel advisors compete to bring you their best personalized quote. It's private, personal, and there's never any obligation.

Secure & reliable

Your account and booking requests are protected and handled through trusted partners. Your name and contact details stay private until you choose to accept a quote.

Personalized service

Real travel advisors, not a faceless booking engine. They help before, during, and after your cruise, every step of the way, and you work directly with the advisor you choose.

Wide selection

Explore sailings from the world's top cruise lines, all in one searchable place. Don't see your sailing? Request any cruise and we'll get advisors working on it.

Advisors compete for you

Submit one request and multiple advisors respond with their best personalized offers. You compare them side by side, with no pricing games and no pressure to book.

No cost, no obligation

Creating an account and requesting quotes is free. You're never obligated to book, and you only move forward when a quote is right for you.

Rated, trusted advisors

Book with confidence. Advisors carry ratings and reviews from real cruisers, so you can see who others have trusted before you choose.
