How Cruise Shoppers works

Cruise Shoppers is a quick, easy way to have trusted travel advisors compete to offer you the best deal on your cruise. No online pricing games, no pressure, and no obligation.

  1. 1

    Find your cruise

    Search sailings across the world's top cruise lines, browse our featured Specials, or if you don't see what you're looking for, request any cruise. We're always adding new options.

  2. 2

    Create a free account

    Sign up in seconds so you can request quotes and see every response in one place. It's free, with no commitment.

  3. 3

    Request a quote

    Tell us the ship, sail date, cabins, guests, and any preferences or discounts that apply. Trusted travel advisors instantly get everything they need to prepare a personalized quote.

  4. 4

    Compare your quotes

    Advisors respond with their best personalized offers. We email you the moment a quote arrives, and you compare them side by side in your account. Your name and contact stay private until you accept a quote, no pricing is shown online, and there's never an obligation to book.

  5. 5

    Choose your advisor and book

    Accept the quote you like best, message your advisor with any questions, and work directly with them by phone or email to lock in your cabin and book your cruise.

It's that easy.
