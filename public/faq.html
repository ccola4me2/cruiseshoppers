Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Cruise Shoppers helps you compare personalized quotes from trusted travel advisors, so you can book with confidence. Here's how it works.

  • ✓ Free for travelers
  • ✓ No obligation
  • ✓ Private until you accept
  • ✓ Verified advisors
What is Cruise Shoppers?

Cruise Shoppers is a cruise marketplace. You search real sailings and request a personalized quote on the cruise you want, and trusted travel advisors respond with their best offers. You compare those offers side by side and choose the one that's right for you. No pricing is shown on the site because every quote is tailored to your trip.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Creating an account, searching sailings, and requesting quotes are completely free for travelers. You are never charged to use Cruise Shoppers.

Am I obligated to book anything?

No. Requesting a quote puts you under no obligation. Compare as many offers as you like, ask questions, and only move forward if an offer is right for you. If nothing fits, you simply don't book.

Is my personal information private?

Yes. When you request a quote, advisors see the sailing and your preferences, but not your name, email, or phone number. Your contact details are shared with a single advisor only when you accept that advisor's quote, so they can finalize your booking. You are never listed publicly. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who are the advisors, and are they vetted?

Advisors are professional travel agents and agencies. To join, each must provide a valid CLIA or IATA / IATAN credential, and every application is reviewed before the advisor can see and respond to quote requests. As you receive quotes, you'll also see each advisor's star rating and reviews from other travelers.

Why don't I see prices on the site?

Cruise Shoppers is quote-based by design. Cruise pricing changes constantly and depends on cabin type, promotions, and group rates an advisor can access. Instead of a stale online price, you get a current, personalized quote, often better than public rates.

How do I get a quote?

Search for the sailing you want (or use Neptune, our AI cruise finder, to describe your ideal trip), then click the sail date to request a personalized quote. Sign in or create a free account, add any preferences, and submit. Advisors are notified and respond with their offers.

How many quotes will I receive?

It varies by sailing and timing. You may receive several offers or just one. Each appears in your My Quotes area, where you can compare fares, inclusions, and advisor ratings in one place.

How do I compare offers?

My Quotes lays your offers out in a comparison view: total fare, what's included, deposits and final payment, onboard credit, and each advisor's rating. When advisors quote multiple cabin types, you can compare fares row by row and see the best value flagged.

What happens when I accept a quote?

When you accept an offer, that advisor receives your contact details and reaches out to finalize the booking. The other advisors are notified that you've chosen a different quote. From there, you work directly with your chosen advisor.

Do I book and pay through Cruise Shoppers?

No. Cruise Shoppers connects you with an advisor; your booking and payment are handled directly by that advisor (and the cruise line), under their terms. Cruise Shoppers is not the seller and never takes payment for a cruise.

Can I message my advisor?

Yes. Once you accept a quote, a secure message thread opens in My Quotes so you can ask questions and coordinate directly with your advisor.

Can I leave a review?

Yes. After you accept a quote and work with an advisor, you can leave a star rating and review. Those ratings help other travelers choose with confidence.

Which cruise lines can I search?

You can search sailings across the world's top cruise lines. Use the filters or ask Neptune to find the line, destination, ship, or dates you have in mind.

How do I get started?

Create a free account, search for a cruise, and request your first quote. It takes just a minute. Sign up free to begin.
