Travel advisor FAQ

Everything you need to know about joining.

What is CruiseShoppers?

CruiseShoppers is a cruise marketplace. Travelers search sailings and request personalized quotes (no pricing is shown on the site), and travel advisors respond with their best offers.

How do I receive leads?

When a traveler requests a quote on a sailing, it appears instantly in your advisor dashboard. Each lead includes the traveler's name, email, and phone, plus the cruise line, ship, sail date, and itinerary.

How do I respond to a request?

Reach out to the traveler directly using the contact details on the lead (an "Email client" button is built in). You then follow up with your personalized pricing and options.

Does it cost anything to join?

Creating an advisor account is free. You can review this policy and any program terms with the CruiseShoppers team.

Who can become an advisor?

The program is for professional travel advisors and agencies. Applying takes a valid CLIA or IATA / IATAN number. Each application is reviewed, and you'll be able to see client quote requests once your account is approved.

Why doesn't the site show prices?

CruiseShoppers is quote-based by design. Travelers browse itineraries without live pricing and request quotes, which is exactly where you come in with a tailored offer.

Is there any commitment?

No long-term commitment to browse leads. Sign up, watch your dashboard, and respond to the requests that fit your business.
